# Techmai Weather Safety Policy

## Outdoor installation
Reschedule outdoor installation when the forecast includes heavy snow, freezing rain, ice storms, or thunderstorms. Reschedule outdoor work when wind reaches 40 km/h or more. An outdoor appointment with an 80 percent or higher precipitation probability should be reviewed for rescheduling.

## Indoor installation
Indoor installation may continue during light rain when travel conditions are safe. Heavy snow, freezing rain, ice storms, thunderstorms, or unsafe travel conditions require rescheduling regardless of service type.

## Forecast timing
Review appointments 48 hours before the scheduled start and review them again 24 hours before the scheduled start when severe weather is possible.
